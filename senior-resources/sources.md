# Senior guide sources and verification

Updated October 26, 2026. This guide is prepared by Christopher Truman as an individual
Torrance resident. It is not an official agency publication. Spanish, Korean, Japanese and
Traditional Chinese text remains a draft translation for fluent-speaker review.

The refresh uses the dashboard repository's October 15 referral sheet, October 17
back-side source corrections, October 18 senior-services research, and October 9 benefits
briefings as discovery leads. Public agency and provider pages were rechecked for this
update. No private correspondence, participant records, voter data, or modeled enrollment
gap estimates were added to the guide.

## Boundaries and maintenance

- Some City transit and County pages restrict automated access. The refresh combines
  current search-index text, available official/provider pages and the dated dashboard
  primary-source checks; it is not a claim that every linked page or application was fully
  downloaded and reviewed anew. The FY27 City application was read directly.
- PACE's office is moving and it does not offer ordinary in-person application intake.
  The guide points to online/paper instructions and says to check before visiting. The
  May instruction sheet's old walk-in hours and daily intake cap are not advertised.
- SCE's standard ESA application is distinct from the Whole Home pilot, which is closing
  and no longer accepts new projects. Only standard ESA is offered by the guide.
- No referral was made, no form was submitted and no agency was contacted. Program
  eligibility is determined by the agency, not this guide.
- Recheck deadlines, provider notices, income tables and contact details before a later
  printing. Review all five languages when updating facts, keeping existing card IDs and
  redirect paths working.
