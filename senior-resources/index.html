Discounts, benefits and local services for Torrance seniors and people with disabilities. Check these programs for help with bills, health care, food, home repairs, taxes and rides.

Bills and utilities

City utility, trash, sewer and water discounts

Who qualifies
Age 62+ or permanently disabled, with household income of $39,900 or less for the prior calendar year (the limit is indexed each year).
What you get
Trash: $15.08 a month instead of $49.14 for a 64-gallon cart. City utility tax exemption, plus qualifying City sewer and water discounts.
How to apply
Use the City Finance FY27 application with proof of prior-year gross household income and utility bills. Renew every 2 years. Questions: (310) 618-5830.
Good to know
The discounted trash rate is for a 64-gallon cart only. If you have a 96-gallon cart, the City will swap it for a 64-gallon cart when you are approved. The tax exemption covers your own home, with the utilities in your name or your qualifying spouse's name.

Electric and gas bill discounts (CARE and FERA)

Who qualifies
For 1–2 people, CARE: household income up to $43,280; FERA: $43,281–$54,100. Larger households have higher limits. Qualifying benefits can also establish CARE eligibility (June 2026–May 2027).
What you get
SCE CARE: 32.5% off electricity; SoCalGas CARE: 20% off gas. SCE FERA: 18% off electricity. Apply separately with each utility.
How to apply
Apply online at sce.com and socalgas.com, or call the number on your bill.

Cal Water Customer Assistance Program (CAP)

Who qualifies
Cal Water customers with qualifying income or participation in programs such as CARE, CalFresh or SSI. For 1–2 people, the income limit is $43,280 (June 2026–May 2027).
What you get
A discount on the water service charge. Check the company on your bill; this is separate from Torrance Municipal Water discounts.
How to apply
Apply with Cal Water using your account number. Questions: (877) 419-1701.

Cal Water CAP

Energy bill help and free home upgrades

Who qualifies
Income-qualified households. LIHEAP and utility Energy Savings Assistance (ESA) have different eligibility rules. Renters and homeowners can ask about help.
What you get
LIHEAP can help pay an energy bill, subject to funding. Standard ESA offers eligible efficiency upgrades at no cost after an assessment.
How to apply
For LIHEAP, apply through PACE online or ask about paper applications: (213) 353-3982. Check current office arrangements before visiting. For standard ESA, apply through SCE or your gas utility.

Medical Baseline: electricity and gas

Who qualifies
A household member with a qualifying medical condition or medical equipment need. Eligibility is based on medical need, not income.
What you get
SCE offers extra electricity at the baseline rate or an 11% discount on plans without a baseline. SoCalGas offers extra gas at its lowest baseline rate.
How to apply
Apply separately with each utility. A medical professional must certify the need. SCE: (800) 655-4555. SoCalGas: (800) 427-2200.

Reduced home or mobile phone costs for one qualifying line per household.

Ask a participating phone provider to enroll you. California LifeLine and federal Lifeline require separate enrollment; ask about both. Application help: (877) 858-7463.

Health and care

Help paying Medicare costs and prescriptions

Who qualifies
People on Medicare with lower incomes. Income and asset rules differ by program; ask HICAP to screen you for Medicare Savings Programs and Part D Extra Help.
What you get
Medicare Savings Programs can pay the Part B premium ($202.90 a month in 2026) and sometimes other costs. Extra Help lowers prescription costs.
How to apply
Free, unbiased HICAP counseling: (800) 434-0222. Apply for Medicare Savings Programs through BenefitsCal or DPSS at (866) 613-3777; apply for Extra Help through Social Security. Medicare plan Open Enrollment: October 15–December 7, 2026.

In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS)

Who qualifies
People age 65+, blind people, or people with disabilities who meet Medi-Cal eligibility requirements and need assistance to remain safely at home.
What you get
Authorized help with personal care, meals and household tasks after a county assessment.
How to apply
Apply through LA County DPSS: (888) 944-4477. A health care certification and an assessment are required.

LA County IHSS

Supplemental Security Income (SSI/SSP)

Who qualifies
People age 65+, blind people, or people with disabilities with limited income and resources. Countable resources are generally limited to $2,000 for one person or $3,000 for a couple; some property is excluded.
What you get
Monthly cash assistance, including California’s state supplement. Your payment depends on income and living arrangements.
How to apply
Ask Social Security to check eligibility: (800) 772-1213 or ssa.gov/ssi. Other eligibility rules also apply.

Social Security SSI

Home repairs

Free home repairs: Home Improvement Program (City of Torrance)

Who qualifies
Age 50+ and/or disabled, owner and occupant of a single-family home in Torrance. Income must meet HUD limits (FY 2026/2027: $58,300 for 1 person, $66,650 for 2).
What you get
Free grab bars, ramps, handrails, light plumbing, and minor repairs. Not covered: major roof repairs, HVAC replacement, painting, or concrete work.
How to apply
Fill out the HIP application with proof of ownership and income. Call (310) 618-2731 or email HIP@TorranceCA.Gov. Reapply each April.

Home repairs: Habitat for Humanity of Greater Los Angeles

Who qualifies
Income-qualified homeowners in Habitat LA’s service area who live in their home. Eligible home types include single-family and manufactured homes, condos, townhomes and duplexes.
What you get
Low-cost health, safety and accessibility repairs. Selection depends on funding, need and home condition.
How to apply
Complete Habitat LA’s screening form. Ask about current income limits and coverage: (310) 323-4663 ext. 314 or homepreservation@habitatla.org. Approval is not guaranteed.

Taxes and tax preparation

Property Tax Postponement (State of California)

Who qualifies
Age 62+, blind, or disabled; you own and live in your home (including many mobile homes); 2025 household income of $57,002 or less; at least 40% equity. You must not have a reverse mortgage. Age 62 must be reached by December 31, 2026, unless qualifying through blindness or disability.
What you get
Put off paying this year's property taxes. The state places a lien on the home, with 5% simple interest, repaid when the home is sold or transferred.
How to apply
Mail the signed application and required documents to the State Controller's Office, P.O. Box 942850, Sacramento, CA 94250-0001. It must be postmarked between October 1, 2026 and February 10, 2027. Funds are limited and applications are processed in the order received, so apply early. Questions: (800) 952-5661, or postponement@sco.ca.gov.

Official pageApplication form (PDF)

Homeowner and Measure W tax exemptions

Who qualifies
The Homeowners’ Exemption is for an owner-occupied principal residence. Measure W’s separate senior exemption requires a qualifying low-income homeowner age 62+ in a single-family residence.
What you get
The Homeowners’ Exemption reduces assessed value by $7,000, not your tax bill by $7,000. Measure W can exempt a qualifying parcel from that special tax.
How to apply
Apply for the Homeowners’ Exemption through the LA County Assessor. Apply separately for Measure W each year using Safe Clean Water LA’s current-year form and deadline.

Apply through Metro LIFE. You can combine LIFE with a senior/disabled Reduced Fare TAP card. Help: (213) 922-2378.

Descuentos municipales de servicios, basura, alcantarillado y agua

Quién califica
62 años o más o con discapacidad permanente, con ingresos brutos del hogar de $39,900 o menos en el año calendario anterior (el límite se ajusta cada año).
Qué ofrece
Basura: $15.08 al mes en lugar de $49.14 para un contenedor de 64 galones. Exención del impuesto municipal de servicios y descuentos de alcantarillado y agua que correspondan.
Cómo solicitar
Use la solicitud FY27 de Finanzas, con comprobantes de ingresos brutos del año anterior y facturas. Renueve cada 2 años. Preguntas: (310) 618-5830.
Conviene saber
La tarifa reducida de basura es solo para un contenedor de 64 galones. Si tiene uno de 96 galones, la Ciudad lo cambiará por uno de 64 galones cuando se apruebe su solicitud. La exención del impuesto cubre solo su propia vivienda, con los servicios a su nombre o al de su cónyuge que califique.
