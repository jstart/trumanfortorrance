Expanded findings

Research snapshot

Reviewed October 26, 2026. This supplement updates the initial bounded search.

New primary-source finding: the announced input deadline

The City Community Development page links its 2026 Second Quarter report to this ArcGIS StoryMap . In the section Single Family Hillside & Coastal Overlay Zone Feedback , node n-9EGdZT explicitly states that the discussion forum would remain open through August 30, 2026 . This resolves the earlier uncertainty: the date is now supported by a City source, not just real-estate commentary.

The same report says comments would help staff evaluate potential standards and procedures for later Council consideration. It does not identify a comments-received total, publish a response-to-comments report, identify retained counsel, present a section-by-section overlay draft or promise a Council return date in that section. The live report may have been updated after the quarter ended; do not use its quarter label to infer when the outreach language first appeared.

The report also embeds the City's Share Your Feedback on Objective Design Standards video . Fresh YouTube metadata records upload date July 16, 2026 and a description advertising an August 30 deadline. Metadata is retained in sources/city_outreach_video_metadata.json ; this is not an independent audio review.

Later official confirmation of outreach

The August 11 Council minutes , printed p.2 / PDF p.4, record Councilmember Lieu encouraging participation in objective development standards for Hillside Overlay and single-family neighborhoods. This supports continued outreach in August, not an amendment or results report.

Can residents find their comments now?

A July 8 RHA post points to the original Civilspace discussion-topic URL. A direct public GET to that old URL returned 404 on October 26. The newer City-linked Zencity project page rendered in a headless browser, but displayed no public comment thread, participation count, results summary or reporting schedule. An update-subscription form appeared. No login was attempted and no form was submitted.

This establishes a limited public findability problem. It does not establish that comments were deleted, lost, ignored or never analyzed. The announced participation deadline has passed; the current ability to submit additional comments was not verified.
